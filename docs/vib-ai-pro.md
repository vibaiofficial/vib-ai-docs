# VIB AI Pro

VIB AI Pro is a subscription plan for users who want more powerful AI features, more usage capacity, and a smoother AI experience inside VIB AI.

Website: https://vibai.com

## What Is VIB AI Pro?

VIB AI Pro is designed to unlock more advanced AI usage inside VIB AI.

With VIB AI Pro, users may receive benefits such as:

- More AI messages
- Access to more powerful AI features
- Priority experience during peak usage
- Early access to selected new features
- Monthly Credits for AI interactions

Benefits may vary depending on product version, platform rules, region, subscription status, and current product configuration.

## Monthly Credits

Current rule:

- VIB AI Pro includes 300 Credits monthly
- These Credits are valid for 1 month
- Unused subscription Credits may expire automatically after the validity period

Credits are used for AI interactions inside VIB AI.

For more details, see:

- [Credits](./credits.md)

## Subscription Renewal

VIB AI Pro may be offered as an auto-renewing subscription.

Depending on the platform used for purchase, subscription billing and renewal may be managed through:

- Apple App Store
- Google Play
- Other supported payment channels

Users should manage subscription cancellation, renewal, and billing settings through the platform where the subscription was purchased.

## Credit Validity

VIB AI Pro monthly Credits are valid for 1 month.

Users should check their Credit balance and expiration information inside the product when available.

Current general rule:

| Credit Type | Validity |
|---|---|
| VIB AI Pro monthly Credits | 1 month |
| Purchased Credits | 1 month |
| Free signup Credits | Permanent |

## Difference Between Pro Credits and Free Credits

Free signup Credits are currently permanent.

VIB AI Pro monthly Credits are subscription-based and valid for 1 month.

When multiple types of Credits are available, VIB AI may use Credits according to product rules, including using free Credits first and using Credits with the nearest expiration date before later-expiring Credits.
