# Data Contribution Center

The Data Contribution Center is where users can participate in AI data contribution activities inside VIB AI.

These activities help support world-model-driven intelligence by contributing real-world, multimodal, and human-aligned data.

Website: https://vibai.com

## What Is the Data Contribution Center?

The Data Contribution Center is a dedicated space for AI data contribution quests.

Users may join available quests, review instructions, complete contribution activities, and track progress based on the rules shown inside the product.

The goal is to help AI systems better understand real-world environments, human feedback, multimodal information, and workflow context.

## Why Data Contribution Matters

Data is the foundation of world-model-driven intelligence.

Through structured data contribution, VIB AI can support AI systems in understanding:

- Real-world objects
- Visual scenes
- Audio information
- Text meaning
- Human feedback
- Environmental context
- State changes
- Real-world scenarios

Human participation helps improve the world model over time.

## Contribution Formats

Available contribution formats may vary depending on product version, region, account status, guild access, and active quests.

Common formats may include:

- Quick Review
- Image Collection
- Audio Collection
- Video Collection
- Text Review
- Other multimodal contribution activities

## Quick Review

Quick Review is a lightweight contribution format.

Users may review simple content and provide structured feedback based on the instructions shown inside the quest.

Quick Review may include formats such as:

- Image Quick Review
- Text Emotion Quick Review
- Simple annotation or feedback activities

Users should follow the on-screen instructions carefully and focus on accuracy.

## Collection

Collection is a contribution format where users may submit real-world data based on quest requirements.

Collection activities may include:

- Image Collection
- Audio Collection
- Video Collection
- Text-based contribution
- Scenario-based contribution

Users should make sure submitted content follows the quest instructions and quality requirements.

## Quest Access

Quests in the Data Contribution Center may be available through different access types.

These may include:

- Public quests
- Guild-based quests
- Region-based quests
- Assigned onboarding quests
- Limited-time quests

Availability may change over time.

Users should refer to the quest list shown inside the product for the most up-to-date information.

## My Quests

After joining or being assigned a quest, users may be able to view it under My Quests.

My Quests may show:

- Joined quests
- Assigned quests
- Quest progress
- Completion status
- Reward status
- Remaining tasks
- Quest instructions

Users should check My Quests regularly to track their participation.

## Quest Progress

Quest Progress helps users understand their current completion status.

Depending on the quest, progress may include:

- Joined days
- Completed days
- Daily completion status
- Claimed rewards
- Remaining rewards
- Task completion records

Users should complete each quest according to the rules shown in the product.

## Quality Expectations

Contribution quality is important.

Users should:

- Read quest instructions carefully
- Submit clear and relevant content
- Avoid duplicate or low-quality submissions
- Complete reviews accurately
- Follow the required format
- Respect privacy and platform rules
- Avoid submitting prohibited or unsafe content

Low-quality or non-compliant submissions may affect progress, rewards, or future quest access.

## Rewards

Some data contribution quests may include rewards.

Reward rules may vary depending on:

- Quest type
- Quest duration
- Completion status
- Quality requirements
- Guild rules
- Platform rules
- Campaign settings

Users should review reward details inside each quest before participating.

## Best Practices

To complete data contribution activities successfully:

- Use the same email across web and app when required
- Confirm the correct quest before starting
- Read all instructions before submission
- Complete tasks accurately
- Check progress after each completion
- Keep notifications enabled when reminders are needed
- Contact support if quest access or progress looks incorrect

## Important Notes

Quest availability, contribution formats, reward rules, and quality requirements may change over time.

The information shown inside the VIB AI product should be considered the most up-to-date reference.
