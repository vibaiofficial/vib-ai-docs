# FAQ

Frequently asked questions about VIB AI.

VIB AI is an intelligent system powered by world models, designed to connect globally collaborative data, world understanding, and AI agents for real-world decision-making.

Website: https://vibai.com

## General

### What is VIB AI?

VIB AI is an intelligent system powered by world models.

It connects AI chat, global data contribution, world-model-driven intelligence, and Smart Task Agents to support real-world decision-making and workflow execution.

### What is VIB World?

VIB World is the world model system behind VIB AI.

It is designed to help AI systems move from data processing to world understanding, including relationships, structure, change, causality, and real-world context.

### What makes VIB AI different from a generic chatbot?

VIB AI is not only focused on conversation.

It is designed around world-model-driven intelligence, global collaborative data, workflow context, traceable task completion, and human-reviewable outputs.

The goal is to help AI move from understanding to judgment and action.

## Account

### How do I create a VIB AI account?

Users can create an account inside VIB AI using the registration flow shown in the product.

A typical flow may include:

1. Open VIB AI
2. Go to the account or profile page
3. Choose registration
4. Enter your email address
5. Enter the verification code
6. Set your password
7. Complete required onboarding steps

### Should I use the same email on web and app?

Yes.

When account sync is needed, users should use the same email address across VIB AI web and app experiences.

Yes.

## Credits

### What are Credits?

Credits represent available AI usage inside VIB AI.

Credits may be used for AI interactions and other supported AI-powered features.

### How can I get Credits?

Users may receive or obtain Credits through:

- Free signup rewards
- VIB AI Pro subscription
- Credit purchases
- Platform rewards
- Quest-related rewards
- Member or activity rewards

### How many free Credits do new users receive?

Current rule:

- Free signup reward: 5 Credits
- Validity: Permanent

### Do Credits expire?

Different Credit types may have different expiration rules.

Current rules:

| Credit Type | Validity |
|---|---|
| Free signup Credits | Permanent |
| VIB AI Pro monthly Credits | 1 month |
| Purchased Credits | 1 month |
| Reward Credits | Based on the reward rule shown in the app |

### Which Credits are used first?

Current general rule:

1. Free Credits are used first
2. Credits with the nearest expiration date are used before Credits with a later expiration date

## Quests

### What are Quests?

Quests are structured AI data contribution activities inside VIB AI.

Users can join quests, follow instructions, complete contribution tasks, track progress, and view reward-related information based on the rules shown inside the product.

### What are onboarding quests?

Onboarding quests are quests assigned to help new users learn how to use VIB AI and complete basic contribution activities.

These quests are usually simple and guided.

### Do onboarding quests require a Quest Deposit?

No.

Onboarding quests do not require a Quest Deposit.

### Do other quests require a Quest Deposit?

Some non-onboarding quests may require a Quest Deposit before participation.

Users should review the Quest Deposit information shown inside each quest before confirming participation.

### What is a Quest Deposit?

A Quest Deposit is a participation guarantee used to support stable, timely, and quality completion of structured AI data contribution quests.

A Quest Deposit is not a platform service fee. It is linked to the user's quest participation and may be returned or adjusted according to the quest rules, completion status, and quality review results shown inside the product.

### Where can I see my joined quests?

After joining or being assigned a quest, users may be able to view it under My Quests.

My Quests may show joined quests, assigned quests, quest progress, completion status, claimed rewards, remaining rewards, deposit status when applicable, and instructions.

### What happens if my submission quality is low?

Low-quality, inaccurate, incomplete, late, duplicate, or non-compliant submissions may affect progress, rewards, Quest Deposit status, or future quest access.

Users should always follow the instructions shown inside each quest.

## Guilds

### What are Guilds?

Guilds are collaboration groups inside the VIB AI ecosystem.

Users may join guilds to participate in organized AI data contribution activities, access guild-based quests, and grow through structured collaboration.

### What is VIB Official?

VIB Official is the official collaboration guild operated by VIB AI.

It may be used to support onboarding, basic contribution training, and early user participation.

### Do guild quests require a Quest Deposit?

Some non-onboarding guild quests may require a Quest Deposit before participation.

Onboarding quests do not require a Quest Deposit.

Users should always review the deposit information shown inside each quest.
