# AI Chat

AI Chat is one of the core features of VIB AI.

VIB AI supports AI-powered conversations for everyday writing, research, translation, planning, summarization, and productivity workflows.

Website: https://vibai.com

## What Is AI Chat?

AI Chat allows users to interact with AI models through natural conversation.

Users can ask questions, generate content, refine ideas, translate text, summarize information, and receive support for everyday knowledge work.

VIB AI is designed to connect AI chat with world-model-driven intelligence, helping users move from simple conversation toward more useful task context and decision support.

## Common Use Cases

Users may use AI Chat for:

- Writing drafts
- Editing and rewriting text
- Translation
- Research support
- Summarization
- Planning
- Brainstorming
- Learning support
- Productivity workflows
- Everyday Q&A

## Model Access

VIB AI may provide access to different AI models or model experiences depending on product version, account status, subscription status, region, and platform rules.

## Best Practices

To get better results from AI Chat:

- Be clear about your goal
- Provide enough context
- Break complex requests into smaller steps
- Review AI-generated content before using it
- Check important information when accuracy matters
- Avoid sharing sensitive personal information unless necessary
- Use follow-up questions to refine the result

## World-Model-Driven Intelligence

VIB AI is built around world-model-driven intelligence.

This means VIB AI is designed not only to process text, but also to support deeper understanding of context, relationships, structure, and change.

AI Chat is one entry point into this broader system.

The long-term goal is to help AI move from conversation to useful judgment, planning, and action.

## Human Review

AI-generated content may be useful, but users should review important outputs before relying on them.

Human review is especially important for:

- Legal content
- Financial content
- Medical content
- Business decisions
- Public-facing content
- Sensitive workflows
- High-impact decisions

VIB AI is designed to support human-AI collaboration, where AI helps users work faster while users remain responsible for final review and decisions.

## Limitations

AI Chat may sometimes produce incomplete, inaccurate, outdated, or unexpected responses.

Users should not treat AI-generated content as professional advice unless verified with qualified sources or professionals.

Availability, model behavior, and feature performance may vary depending on product version, model provider, region, and usage conditions.
