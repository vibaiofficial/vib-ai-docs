# Credits

Credits are used for AI interactions inside VIB AI.

This guide explains how Credits work, how users may receive Credits, and how Credits are used in the product.

Website: https://vibai.com

## What Are Credits?

Credits represent available AI usage inside VIB AI.

When users interact with AI models, Credits may be deducted based on the type of interaction, model access, product feature, or usage rules shown in the app.

Credits help users access AI-powered features such as:

- AI chat
- Writing support
- Research assistance
- Translation
- Planning
- Summarization
- Productivity workflows
- Other supported AI interactions

## How Users Can Get Credits

Users may receive or obtain Credits through different methods.

These may include:

- Free signup Credits
- VIB AI Pro subscription
- Credit purchases
- Platform rewards
- Quest-related rewards
- Member or activity rewards

Availability may vary depending on region, account status, product version, platform rules, and active campaigns.

## Free Signup Credits

New users may receive free signup Credits.

Current rule:

- Free signup reward: 5 Credits
- Validity: Permanent

These Credits do not expire under the current rule.

## VIB AI Pro Credits

VIB AI Pro may include monthly Credits as part of the subscription benefits.

Current rule:

- VIB AI Pro includes 300 Credits monthly
- Validity: 1 month

Subscription Credits are valid for 1 month and may expire automatically if unused within the validity period.

For more details, see:

- [VIB AI Pro](./vib-ai-pro.md)

## Purchased Credits

Users may be able to purchase additional Credit packs.

Current rule:

- Purchased Credits are valid for 1 month
- Unused purchased Credits may expire automatically after the validity period

Credit pack availability, amount, price, and payment method may vary depending on the platform and region.

## Reward Credits

Users may receive Credits through platform rewards or activities.

Examples may include:

- Sign-in rewards
- Member rewards
- Quest-related rewards
- Other promotional activities

Reward details may vary depending on the active campaign and product rules shown in the app.

## Credit Usage Order

When multiple types of Credits are available, VIB AI may apply a usage order based on validity and product rules.

Current general rule:

1. Free Credits are used first
2. Credits with the nearest expiration date are used before Credits with a later expiration date

This helps users reduce the risk of expiring Credits.

## Credit Expiration

Different Credit types may have different expiration rules.

Current rules:

| Credit Type | Validity |
|---|---|
| Free signup Credits | Permanent |
| VIB AI Pro monthly Credits | 1 month |
| Purchased Credits | 1 month |
| Reward Credits | Based on the reward rule shown in the app |

Users should check their Credit balance and expiration details inside the product when available.

## Important Notes

Credit rules may vary depending on:

- Product version
- Account status
- Subscription status
- Platform rules
- App Store or Google Play requirements
- Regional availability
- Active campaigns or rewards

The Credit balance, deduction rules, and expiration information shown inside the product should be considered the most up-to-date reference.
