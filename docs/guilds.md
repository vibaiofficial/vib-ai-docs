# Guilds

Guilds are collaboration groups inside the VIB AI ecosystem.

Users may join guilds to participate in organized AI data contribution activities, access guild-based quests, and grow through structured collaboration.

Website: https://vibai.com

## What Are Guilds?

Guilds are groups that help organize users around AI data contribution quests and collaboration activities.

A guild may help users:

- Join structured contribution activities
- Access guild-based quests
- Learn how to complete quests correctly
- Improve contribution quality
- Collaborate with other users
- Build participation experience over time

Guild availability, access rules, and permissions may vary depending on product version, region, guild settings, and platform rules.

## VIB Official Guild

VIB Official is the official collaboration guild operated by VIB AI.

It may be used to support onboarding, basic contribution training, and early user participation.

Users may be guided to join VIB Official when completing onboarding flows or assigned quests.

## Guild-Based Quests

Some quests may be available through guild access.

Guild-based quests may include:

- Onboarding quests
- Quick Review quests
- Collection quests
- Assigned contribution activities
- Limited-time guild quests
- Region-based or eligibility-based quests

Users should review each quest page for the most up-to-date participation, reward, quality, and deposit requirements.

## Joining a Guild

A typical guild joining flow may include:

1. Open VIB AI
2. Go to the Guild or Quests area
3. Find the target guild
4. Enter an invite code if required
5. Submit a join request
6. Wait for approval when applicable
7. Access available guild-based quests

Some guilds may require invite codes, approval, eligibility checks, or platform assignment.

## Guild Roles

Guilds may support different roles depending on product configuration.

These may include:

- Guild leader
- Vice leader
- Members
- New members
- Experienced contributors

Role names, permissions, and responsibilities may vary by guild and platform rules.

## Member Levels

Guilds may use member levels to support structured growth and quality control.

Member levels may help indicate:

- Contribution experience
- Quest completion history
- Quality performance
- Access to more advanced quests
- Ability to support or manage other members

The exact level system may vary depending on product configuration and guild rules.

## Guild Rules

Each guild may have its own participation rules.

Guild rules may cover:

- How users join
- Which quests members can access
- Contribution expectations
- Quality requirements
- Member roles
- Reward-related rules
- Referral or commission rules, when applicable
- Internal management policies

Users should follow the rules shown inside the product and the relevant guild.

## Rewards and Commissions

Some guild activities may include rewards or referral-related commissions.

Reward and commission rules may vary depending on:

- Guild configuration
- Quest type
- Quest completion status
- Quality result
- Member role
- Platform rules
- Active campaigns

Users should review the reward details shown inside the product.

When referral commissions are available, they may depend on eligible guild quests and guild-specific rules.

## Quest Deposit and Guild Quests

Some non-onboarding guild quests may require a Quest Deposit before participation.

Current general rule:

- Onboarding quests do not require a Quest Deposit
- Non-onboarding quests may require a Quest Deposit
- Deposit requirements may vary by quest, guild, duration, difficulty, region, and platform configuration

Users should always review the Quest Deposit information shown inside each quest before joining.

For more details, see:

- [Quests](./quests.md)

## Best Practices

To participate in guild activities successfully:

- Use the same account across web and app when required
- Join the correct guild
- Read guild rules carefully
- Review quest instructions before starting
- Complete quests accurately and on time
- Track progress regularly
- Follow quality standards
- Contact support if guild access or quest progress looks incorrect

## Important Notes

Guild features, access rules, roles, rewards, commissions, and quest availability may change over time.

The information shown inside the VIB AI product should be considered the most up-to-date reference.

If there is a difference between this public guide and the product interface, the product interface should be considered the most up-to-date reference.
