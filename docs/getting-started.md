# Getting Started with VIB AI

Welcome to VIB AI.

VIB AI is an intelligent system powered by world models, designed to connect globally collaborative data, world understanding, and AI agents for real-world decision-making.

This guide introduces the basic ways to get started with VIB AI.

Website: https://vibai.com

## What You Can Do with VIB AI

VIB AI brings together AI chat, world-model-driven intelligence, and data contribution workflows.

With VIB AI, users can:

- Chat with AI models
- Explore AI-powered writing, research, translation, and planning
- Use Credits for AI interactions
- Join quests
- Complete data contribution activities
- Participate in guild-based collaboration
- Track quest progress and rewards

## 1. Create an Account

To start using VIB AI, create an account with your email address.

A typical sign-up flow may include:

1. Open VIB AI
2. Go to the account or profile page
3. Choose registration
4. Enter your email address
5. Enter the verification code
6. Set your password
7. Complete the required onboarding steps

Please make sure you use the same email address across VIB AI web and app experiences when account sync is needed.

## 2. Explore AI Chat

VIB AI supports AI-powered conversations for everyday productivity and knowledge workflows.

You can use AI chat for:

- Writing
- Research
- Translation
- Planning
- Summarization
- Idea generation
- Everyday productivity support

Credits may be used when interacting with AI models.

## 3. Understand Credits

Credits are used for AI interactions inside VIB AI.

Credits may be obtained through:

- Free signup rewards
- Subscriptions
- Credit purchases
- Platform rewards
- Quest-related rewards

Credit availability, usage, and expiration may vary depending on the account type, subscription status, reward type, and platform rules.

For more details, see:

- [Credits](./credits.md)
- [VIB AI Pro](./vib-ai-pro.md)

## 4. Open the Data Contribution Center

The Data Contribution Center is where users can participate in AI data contribution activities.

These activities help support world-model-driven intelligence by contributing real-world, multimodal, and human-aligned data.

Depending on availability, users may see different contribution formats, such as:

- Quick Review
- Image Collection
- Audio Collection
- Video Collection
- Text Review
- Other structured data contribution activities

For more details, see:

- [Data Contribution Center](./data-contribution-center.md)
- [Quests](./quests.md)

## 5. Join Quests

Quests are structured contribution activities inside VIB AI.

A quest may include:

- A quest title
- A quest description
- Instructions
- Duration
- Progress tracking
- Reward information
- Completion requirements

Users should carefully read the quest details before joining or completing a quest.

For more details, see:

- [Quests](./quests.md)

## 6. Participate in Guilds

Guilds are collaboration groups inside the VIB AI ecosystem.

A guild may help users:

- Join organized contribution activities
- Access guild-based quests
- Collaborate with other users
- Build contribution experience
- Participate in structured data collaboration

For more details, see:

- [Guilds](./guilds.md)

## 7. Track Progress

After joining a quest, users may be able to track their progress inside the product.

Progress information may include:

- Joined quests
- Quest progress
- Completion status
- Claimed rewards
- Remaining rewards
- Daily or period-based task status

Users should follow the instructions shown in the product to complete each quest correctly.

## 8. Best Practices

To get the best experience with VIB AI:

- Use the same email across web and app when required
- Read quest instructions carefully
- Complete contribution activities accurately
- Follow quality standards
- Keep app notifications enabled when task reminders are needed
- Review progress regularly
- Contact support if you experience account, quest, or reward issues
