# Quests

Quests are structured AI data contribution activities inside VIB AI.

Users can join quests, follow instructions, complete contribution tasks, track progress, and view reward-related information based on the rules shown inside the product.

Website: https://vibai.com

## What Are Quests?

Quests are guided activities that help users contribute data, feedback, or review signals to support world-model-driven intelligence.

A quest may include:

- Quest title
- Quest description
- Instructions
- Duration
- Difficulty level
- Contribution format
- Completion requirements
- Progress tracking
- Reward information
- Quest Deposit requirements, when applicable

Quest availability may vary depending on product version, region, guild access, account status, and active campaigns.

## Quest Categories

VIB AI may display quests in different categories depending on product configuration.

Common categories may include:

- Ongoing
- Joined
- Guild-based quests
- Assigned onboarding quests
- Global and regional quests

Users should check the quest list inside the product for the most up-to-date availability.

## Common Quest Types

Quest types may vary over time.

Common formats may include:

### Quick Review

Quick Review is a lightweight review format where users provide simple structured feedback based on quest instructions.

Examples may include:

- Image Quick Review
- Text Emotion Quick Review
- Simple review or annotation activities

### Collection

Collection quests ask users to contribute real-world data based on the quest requirements.

Examples may include:

- Image Collection
- Audio Collection
- Video Collection
- Text-based contribution
- Scenario-based contribution

### Onboarding Quest

Onboarding quests may be assigned to help new users learn how to use VIB AI and complete basic contribution activities.

These quests are usually designed to be simple and guided.

Onboarding quests do not require a Quest Deposit.

## Quest Deposit

Some non-onboarding quests may require a Quest Deposit before participation.

A Quest Deposit is a participation guarantee used to support stable, timely, and quality completion of structured AI data contribution quests.

Current general rule:

- Onboarding quests do not require a Quest Deposit
- Non-onboarding quests may require a Quest Deposit before joining
- Deposit requirements may vary by quest type, difficulty, duration, region, guild rules, and platform configuration
- Users should review the Quest Deposit information shown inside each quest before confirming participation

A Quest Deposit is not a platform service fee. It is linked to the user's quest participation and may be returned or adjusted according to the quest rules, completion status, and quality review results shown inside the product.

## Quest Details

Before joining or completing a quest, users should review the quest details carefully.

Quest details may include:

- What the quest is about
- How long the quest lasts
- How progress is counted
- How rewards are calculated
- Whether a Quest Deposit is required
- What quality standards apply
- What actions may cause failure or rejection

The information shown inside the quest page should be treated as the most up-to-date reference.

## Joining a Quest

A typical quest flow may include:

1. Open VIB AI
2. Go to the Quests page or Data Contribution Center
3. Select an available quest
4. Read the quest information
5. Check whether the quest requires a Quest Deposit
6. Confirm participation
7. Complete the required contribution activity
8. Track progress and reward status

Some quests may require guild access, invitation, eligibility review, assignment by the platform, or a Quest Deposit before participation.

## My Quests

After joining or being assigned a quest, users may be able to view it under My Quests.

My Quests may show:

- Joined quests
- Assigned quests
- Ongoing quests
- Quest progress
- Completion status
- Claimed rewards
- Remaining rewards
- Quest Deposit status, when applicable
- Instructions and details

Users should check My Quests regularly to make sure their progress is recorded correctly.

## Quest Progress

Quest Progress helps users understand their current participation status.

Depending on the quest, progress may include:

- Joined days
- Completed days
- Daily completion status
- Total completion status
- Claimed rewards
- Remaining rewards
- Unfinished tasks
- Not completed status
- Deposit-related status, when applicable

Users should follow the product instructions to complete each quest correctly.

Some quests may include rewards.

Reward rules may vary depending on:

- Quest type
- Difficulty level
- Duration

Users should review the reward details inside each quest before participating.

## Quality Requirements

Quest quality is important.

Users should:

- Read instructions before starting
- Submit clear and relevant content
- Avoid duplicate submissions
- Avoid low-quality or unrelated content
- Follow format requirements
- Complete reviews accurately
- Respect privacy and platform rules
- Avoid prohibited or unsafe content

Low-quality, inaccurate, incomplete, late, duplicate, or non-compliant submissions may affect progress, rewards, Quest Deposit status, or future quest access.

## Quest Completion

A quest may be considered completed when the user meets the completion requirements shown inside the product.

Completion requirements may include:

- Finishing required daily activities
- Meeting minimum quality standards
- Submitting required content
- Passing review or validation
- Completing the required quest period
- Meeting deposit-related participation rules, when applicable

The exact completion rules may vary by quest.

## Important Notes

Quest information may change based on product version, region, platform rules, and active campaigns.

Quest Deposit requirements may also vary by quest and should always be reviewed before participation.

Users should always follow the instructions shown inside VIB AI.

If there is a difference between this public guide and the product interface, the product interface should be considered the most up-to-date reference.
