# VIB AI Docs

**Public Documentation for VIB AI**

This repository contains public documentation, user guides, and product resources for VIB AI.

VIB AI is an intelligent system powered by world models, designed to connect globally collaborative data, world understanding, and AI agents for real-world decision-making.

Website: https://vibai.com

## Getting Started

VIB AI helps users explore AI chat, world-model-driven intelligence, and data contribution workflows in one platform.

Users can:

- Chat with AI models
- Use Credits for AI interactions
- Join quests
- Complete data contribution tasks
- Participate in guild-based collaboration
- Track quest progress and rewards

## Key Product Areas

### AI Chat

VIB AI supports AI-powered conversations for everyday writing, research, translation, planning, and productivity workflows.

### Credits

Credits are used for AI interactions inside VIB AI.

Credits may be obtained through free signup rewards, subscriptions, purchases, or platform rewards.

### VIB AI Pro

VIB AI Pro unlocks more powerful AI features, more usage capacity, and access to advanced product experiences.

### Data Contribution Center

The Data Contribution Center allows users to join AI data contribution quests and help improve world-model-driven intelligence.

### Quests

Quests are structured AI data contribution activities.

Quest types may include Quick Review, Collection, and other multimodal contribution formats.

### Guilds

Guilds are collaboration groups where users can join quests, participate in organized contribution activities, and grow through structured participation.
